# Requirements

## Goals and stakeholders

Document confirmed stakeholder goals and observable outcomes.

## Functional requirements

Capture confirmed requirements with their source and acceptance criteria. Use stable IDs only if the project adopts an identifier scheme; link detailed specifications instead of repeating flows.

## Non-functional requirements

Record measurable requirements, their rationale, measurement methods and test environment assumptions. Do not invent targets for latency, throughput, availability or security.

## Open questions

List unresolved decisions with owners or reference their canonical discussion records.
